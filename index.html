Ronald McDonald House Family Impact Fund

The Ronald McDonald House Family Impact Fund is a bold initiative to imagine a world where every family has what they need to ensure the best health outcomes for their children. A gift to the Family Impact Fund will help Ronald McDonald House move closer to this vision, enabling us to serve even more families, and serve them better, throughout their child’s healthcare journey.

Accelerating Our Vision

Throughout 50 years of service, Ronald McDonald House has become a trusted leader in family-centered care. Ronald McDonald House is essential to removing barriers that can interfere with families’ ability to access critical pediatric care, participate in treatment decisions, and collaborate with healthcare providers to achieve the best health outcomes for their children.
This work is increasingly urgent—and the need for our programs is growing across the globe. The Family Impact Fund is the fuel which will enable Ronald McDonald House to meet the growing demand for our programs, inspiring support for initiatives that are essential for our continued impact. We are asking donors to invest in the following strategic areas of focus:
  Serve More Families: Growing the presence of Ronald McDonald House programs around the world, near hospitals that are hubs for specialized pediatric healthcare. Our goal is to double the number of families we are able to serve by 2030.
  Serve Families Better: Expanding proven services that are essential to supporting high-quality and effective health care for very sick or injured children, including access to nutritious meals and psycho-social resources.
  Global Research & Innovation: Developing our global research program to better understand family needs globally and drive continuous innovation.
  Capacity & Leadership: Enhancing the leadership of the people who power the Ronald McDonald House mission at local Chapters all over the world.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Ronald McDonald House Family Impact Fund?

The Ronald McDonald House Family Impact Fund is a global nonprofit initiative designed to support families with children undergoing medical care. It aims to improve health outcomes by expanding access to critical pediatric healthcare and offering essential services across the world.

How does my donation make an impact?

Your donation to the Family Impact Fund directly supports Ronald McDonald House’s programs, helping to serve more families, enhance available services like nutritious meals and psycho-social support, and fuel research and leadership capacity. Every contribution helps remove barriers and offer families a better healthcare journey.

What are the main goals of the Family Impact Fund?

The fund focuses on four key areas: serving more families by expanding RMHC’s global presence, enhancing support for families through proven programs, advancing global research and innovation, and boosting leadership capacity at local chapters.

Can I contact Ronald McDonald House to learn more or ask questions?

Yes! You can reach out via the contact page on their website to inquire about the Family Impact Fund or discover how you can support family-centered healthcare initiatives.

Is my donation to the Family Impact Fund tax-deductible?

Yes, donations made to Ronald McDonald House and the Family Impact Fund are tax-deductible as allowed by law. Please consult your local regulations or tax advisor for specifics.

How can I get involved beyond donating?

There are many ways to get involved! You can volunteer, share impact stories, follow RMHC on social media, sign up for email updates, or explore other ways of supporting family-centered care through the Ronald McDonald House network.

Ronald McDonald House Family Impact Fund FAQ

What is the Ronald McDonald House Family Impact Fund and how does it differ from other nonprofit pediatric healthcare programs?
The Ronald McDonald House Family Impact Fund is a global initiative focused on supporting families with sick or injured children by providing housing, nutritious meals, and psycho-social resources near hospitals. Unlike many nonprofits, RMH emphasizes family-centered care, seeks to double its service outreach by 2030, and invests in research and innovation to continuously improve support for families worldwide.
How can I donate to the Family Impact Fund, and what types of donations are accepted?
You can donate to the Family Impact Fund online via credit card, PayPal, or Venmo, as well as by check, money order, or wire transfer. RMH also accepts donations of stock, non-cash assets, and cryptocurrency through The Giving Block. Donor-advised funds and corporate matching are also supported, making giving flexible and inclusive.
Is my donation to the Ronald McDonald House Family Impact Fund tax-deductible?
Yes, donations to the Ronald McDonald House Family Impact Fund are tax-deductible. RMH Global is a registered 501(c)(3) public charity under EIN 36-2934689. Donors are advised to consult with their tax advisor for specifics on their contributions.
What are the main goals of the Family Impact Fund that set it apart from similar organizations?
The Family Impact Fund aims to double the number of families served globally by 2030, expand programs to new regions like India and Brazil, enhance meal and psycho-social offerings, and invest in global research and leadership at local chapters. This strategic, measurable approach distinguishes it from other healthcare nonprofits.
How does the Ronald McDonald House ensure transparency and accountability compared to market alternatives?
RMH provides access to annual reports, financials, leadership details, and program impact metrics. Information about trademarks, governance, and partnerships is made available, ensuring donors and supporters are informed and confident in the organization’s stewardship and mission.
Can I get involved beyond donating, and what engagement opportunities does RMH offer?
Absolutely! RMH encourages volunteering, participating in fundraising events, joining giving collectives, and supporting wishlists. Corporations can become partners, and individuals can follow RMH on social media or sign up for email updates to stay connected and engaged.
